The distinction that matters

Validity is not the same as self-proof

An electronic will can be valid without a qualified custodian — but to be self-proving, the will must be held by a qualified custodian. Does a Florida e-will need a custodian?

For self-proof, yes — and the custodian carries real obligations:

Custodian requirement

$250,000  bond or E&O coverage

A qualified custodian must post a $250,000 bond or maintain errors-and-omissions coverage. TODO Exact statutory framing from the Florida entry.

What trips up attorneys in Florida?

The vulnerable-adult exclusion. Remote execution is not available for testators who qualify as vulnerable adults — and Florida requires screening for this before a remote session. TODO Exact exclusion scope from the Florida entry.
