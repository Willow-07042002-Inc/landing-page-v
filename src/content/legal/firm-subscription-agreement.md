## 1. Definitions

> **Summary:** These terms have the same meaning in this Agreement, the Member Terms and the Privacy Policy.

1.1 "**Access Instructions**" means a Member's directions, given through the Services' separate access-instructions tool, about which documents may be released to which people, and on what trigger.

1.2 "**Attorney**" means an attorney admitted to practice law in at least one U.S. jurisdiction who is a partner, member, employee or of-counsel of the Firm and is designated by the Firm as an Authorized User.

1.3 "**Authorized User**" means an Attorney or a member of the Firm's staff whom the Firm authorizes to access the Services under the Firm's account.

1.4 "**Designated Recipient**" means a person whom a Member designates to receive access to some or all of the Member's documents, whether during the Member's lifetime or on a trigger set out in the Access Instructions.

1.5 "**Feature Terms**" means additional terms that Willow presents for a specific feature and that the Firm accepts before using that feature.

1.6 "**Fiduciary**" means an executor, personal representative or administrator of an estate; a trustee or successor trustee; an agent under a power of attorney; a health care agent, proxy or surrogate; a guardian or conservator; and any other person whom Willow reasonably determines holds a similar legal role for a Member or a Member's estate.

1.7 "**Firm Customer Data**" means all data, documents and information that the Firm or its Authorized Users submit to the Services, including information about the Firm's clients and prospective clients, and the outputs the Services generate from it, such as AI Summaries and other results of AI Analysis (Section 5.1).

1.8 "**Firm Internal Data**" means Firm Customer Data that the Firm keeps for its own use and does not share with a Member, such as attorney notes, unshared documents and AI Summaries that have not been approved. Firm Internal Data is never visible to Members and is not kept in a Member Account after the Member disconnects from the Firm or the Agreement ends.

1.9 "**AI Summary**" means a draft summary of an estate planning document or any part of it, including a summary of trust terms or a description of a "what-if" scenario, that the Services prepare using AI Analysis (Section 5.1) for review by an Attorney.

1.10 "**Member**" means an individual who holds a Willow account for their own estate planning (a "**Member Account**") and who has accepted the Member Terms, whether or not any documents have been added to the account yet. A client of the Firm whom the Firm has invited but who has not yet accepted the Member Terms is an "**Invited Client**."

1.11 "**Member Terms**" means Willow's terms of service for Members, Designated Recipients and Fiduciaries, as updated from time to time.

1.12 "**Order Form**" means an ordering document from Willow, signed by both Parties, that states the plan, fees and any special terms.

1.13 "**Services**" means Willow's web-based platform and related services for storing, organizing, analyzing, summarizing and sharing copies of estate planning documents, managing client accounts, scheduling consultations and coordinating the release of documents to Designated Recipients and Fiduciaries, as described in the Order Form and on Willow's website, and as updated from time to time.

1.14 "**Subscription Term**" means the period during which the Firm is subscribed to the Services, as set out in Section 12.

1.15 "**AI Analysis**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

## 2. The Services

> **Summary:** Willow gives the Firm access to its platform for the Subscription Term. Willow is a software provider, not a law firm, and doesn't give legal advice or refer clients. Willow can improve the Services over time, but won't take away core features during the Firm's current term, except where law, a third-party provider or security requires it.

2.2 **Willow is not a law firm.** Willow is not a law firm, does not practice law and does not provide legal advice. No attorney-client relationship forms between Willow and the Firm, any Authorized User or any client of the Firm. Use of the Services, including a booking or message sent through a Firm's booking page, does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship between the Firm and any person; that relationship forms only on the terms the Firm agrees with its client.

2.3 **No referrals or fee-sharing.** Willow does not recommend or refer clients to the Firm in exchange for payment, does not operate a lawyer referral service, and does not receive any portion of the Firm's legal fees. Willow's fees are fixed under the Order Form and owed regardless of what the Firm charges or collects from its clients. Willow does not recommend, rank or give paid placement to any attorney, including after a Member disconnects from the Firm, and any attorney directory or suggestion Willow offers to Members who have no attorney connected is neutral, is not paid for by any attorney or firm, and is not a referral or recommendation.

2.4 **Changes to the Services.** Willow may update, modify or improve the Services from time to time. Willow will not materially reduce the core functionality of the Services the Firm has subscribed to during the then-current Subscription Term, except where required by law, a third-party provider or security considerations, in which case Willow will give the Firm as much notice as reasonably practicable.

2.5 **Feature Terms.** Some features, including features Willow adds after the Effective Date, may require the Firm to accept Feature Terms before use. Feature Terms describe what the feature does, what data it uses and anything in this Agreement that works differently for that feature, and apply only to the feature they cover.

2.6 **Beta features.** Features Willow labels as beta, preview or early access are provided "as is" for evaluation, may be changed or discontinued at any time, and are excluded from any service commitments.

2.7 **Signing Services.** Willow may offer features for signing, witnessing or notarizing documents ("**Signing Services**") only in states where the law allows it, and only once Willow states on the page of its website that lists where the Services and their features are available (the "**availability page**") that the feature is offered in that state. 2.8 **Lead and intake forms.** Information that prospective clients and others submit through the Firm's lead or intake forms in the Services is Firm Customer Data. Clients pay Willow directly, in the app, for their own Plan; the Firm pays only its subscription. Willow may change how and where Branding appears, and may add further kinds of Firm content to the Services, without amending this Agreement. The Firm will invite to the Services, and upload documents for, only clients who reside in a state listed on the availability page (Section 2.7).

4.2 **Client Plans.** Each Member buys and pays for their own Willow plan directly from Willow, in the Services, under the Member Terms (a "**Plan**"). The Firm pays only its own subscription fee. A Member Account is free, and the Services offer a Plan to a Member once an Attorney is connected to the Member Account. Without a Plan, a Member can view and download copies of the documents the Firm sends to their Member Account and see what others share with them. A Member needs an active Plan to see the AI Summaries the Firm has approved, to share documents with anyone and to add their own documents or supporting files, as described in the Member Terms. People a Member shares with never pay to see what was shared with them; a release request for an account whose Plan has lapsed may carry a reasonable fee under the Member Terms, shown before the request is submitted. Unless the Order Form provides otherwise, the Firm does not pay for or collect fees for any Member's Plan.

4.3 **Invited Clients.** Information the Firm uploads about an Invited Client is Firm Customer Data held only for the Firm. Willow will not contact an Invited Client except to send the invitation and reminders the Firm requests.

4.4 **If the Firm's subscription ends.** If the Firm cancels, or Willow terminates the Agreement for non-payment or breach:

- (b) each Member keeps their Member Account, their documents and any Plan. Willow will tell each connected Member in advance that the Firm no longer offers Willow, that the Member may ask the Firm for a copy of their file, and that the Member's account continues, and will then end the Member's connection to the Firm, with the effects described in Section 4.5. The notice will not recommend any other attorney, although Willow may later suggest attorneys to a Member who has no attorney connected, as described in Section 2.3; and
- (c) the Firm may obtain the export described in Section 7.5, to satisfy its own record-keeping obligations.

4.5 **If a Member disconnects from the Firm.** A Member may end their connection with the Firm at any time by asking Willow or in any other way the Services offer. When a Member asks Willow, Willow may ask the Firm to remove the Member as a client, and the Firm agrees to do so promptly. The Firm may also end it by removing the Member as a client, and remains responsible for any notice its professional obligations require. When the Firm removes a Member, Willow sends the Member, on the Firm's behalf and in the name of the Attorney, a short notice of the disconnection, which does not replace any notice the Firm's professional obligations require. Willow may end it under Section 4.4. When the connection ends (the "**disconnection date**"):

- (a) Willow notifies the Firm, and the Firm can no longer see that Member Account, including anything added after the disconnection date. The Firm keeps a read-only record of the Member as a former client, as attorneys are required to: the Member's contact details and the Firm's relationship notes; the documents that were in the Member Account, with their approved AI Summaries and files, including documents from an earlier firm, and documents the Firm uploaded but never shared, as files without draft summaries; the Member's team and, if the Member shared them, the Member's contacts; the Firm's own family entries and notes; and the messages, posts, surveys and check-ins the Firm sent the Member. Raw AI extractions are not kept. The record is available in the Firm's history in the Services and in every export under Section 7.5 for as long as this Agreement continues;
- (b) the documents the Firm shared with the Member stay in the Member Account as the Member's own copies. Firm Internal Data is never visible to Members and is not part of the Member Account (Section 1.8);
- (c) AI Summaries the Firm approved stay in the Member Account as last approved, and are not updated after the disconnection date;
- (d) the Member's sharing choices and Access Instructions keep working. Release requests go to the Member's new attorney if the Member connects one, and otherwise Willow handles them under the Member Terms;
- (e) the Firm's emails and other messages to that Member through the Services stop, and the Firm's Branding is no longer shown in that Member Account; and
- (f) the Member may connect a different firm, which then sees the Member's whole Member Account, including the documents and AI Summaries the Firm shared with the Member; a couple link stays in place; and a document the Member held jointly with another Member becomes a separate copy in each account.

Any refund of fees the Member paid to the Firm is a matter between the Member and the Firm. The Member's Plan with Willow is not affected by the disconnection.

4.6 **No solicitation of the Firm's clients.** While a Member is connected to the Firm, Willow will not market the services of any other attorney or law firm to that Member. Willow may send the Member service, security, billing and account communications, and the notices described in Sections 4.4 and 4.5.

Attorneys cannot change a Member's sharing choices. Willow is responsible for operating the release tools and for verifying requests for Members who have no Attorney connected. 4.8 **Copies, not originals.** The Services store copies of documents. Willow does not hold original wills, is not a will depository, does not certify any document as a true copy, and does not file or deposit documents with any court. The Firm remains responsible for the custody of any originals it holds. 4.9 **Revoked or replaced documents.** The Firm remains responsible for advising its clients on how to revoke or replace a document, and for updating the documents and sharing in a connected Member Account when a document is replaced. Where the Services offer a way to mark a document as revoked or no longer current, only an Attorney may use it; the marking tells the people the Member has shared the document with that it is no longer current, is not a legal revocation and has no legal effect on the document, and the Services say so to Members and recipients.

## 5. AI Analysis and Connected Services

> **Summary:** Willow's AI analyzes the documents an Attorney uploads or manages: it identifies what each document is and the people and key terms in it, and drafts summaries. An Attorney must review each summary before a client sees it, and the Firm is responsible for what it approves and shares. No AI model is trained on the Firm's data. Connections to calendar, email, contacts and video services are optional and used only for the features the Firm turns on.

5.1 **What the AI does.** The Services use artificial intelligence to analyze documents that an Attorney uploads or manages, to classify them, identify the people and key terms in them, and prepare draft summaries for Attorney review ("**AI Analysis**"). For example, AI Analysis may identify the type of document, the people who made it, their spouse and their address, and use that information to set up a client record for the Firm, which can happen before the client has a Willow account. It may also identify trust terms, describe "what-if" scenarios and summarize individual sections. Willow may improve AI Analysis within this description without changing this Agreement. Any AI feature that goes beyond it, such as drafting assistance, will be offered under its own Feature Terms.

5.2 **Attorney review.** AI Summaries and other results of AI Analysis are drafts. They may be incomplete or inaccurate, and they are not legal advice. The Services do not show an AI Summary to a Member until an Attorney approves it, and then only as Section 4.2 allows. The Firm will ensure that an Attorney reviews each AI Summary before approving it or relying on it, and that client records and other information the Services set up from AI Analysis are checked before the Firm relies on them. The Firm is solely responsible for any AI Summary it approves, edits or shares, and for any advice given to its clients.

5.3 **Consent to AI processing.** The Firm authorizes Willow to process Firm Customer Data using the AI providers listed on Willow's subprocessor page, solely to perform AI Analysis and provide the Services.

5.4 **No training.** Willow will not use Firm Customer Data, or the content of any Member's documents, to train or fine-tune any artificial intelligence model, and its contracts with AI providers prohibit them from doing so. Willow may improve the Services by refining its own instructions and summary components, informed by the kinds of edits Attorneys make, provided that no Firm Customer Data is retained in those instructions or components. Willow may also keep, for the Firm's own use, preferences and style instructions that the Firm's Attorneys set or that reflect their edits, so that AI Summaries for the Firm follow the Firm's preferred approach. Those preferences are Firm Customer Data, are used only for the Firm, and are deleted with the Firm's other data when the Agreement ends.

5.5 **AI providers.** Willow's AI providers process Firm Customer Data only as subprocessors under the DPA, to return the results of AI Analysis to Willow. Their contracts with Willow prohibit them from using it to train their models and require them to keep it confidential. Any temporary retention by an AI provider, including for safety monitoring or legal compliance, is governed by those contracts, and Willow describes its current configuration on its subprocessor page.

5.6 **No AI Analysis without an Attorney.** The Services do not perform AI Analysis on documents in Member Accounts that have no Attorney connected.

5.7 **Connected Services.** Authorized Users may choose to connect third-party applications, accounts and data sources to the Services, such as calendar, email, contacts, video conferencing, practice management, document management and database services (each a "**Connected Service**"). Willow lists the Connected Services it currently offers on its website and may add, change or retire them at any time. For each Connected Service the Firm enables:

- (a) the Firm authorizes Willow to access, retrieve, send and synchronize data between the Services and the Connected Service as needed to provide the features the Firm turns on, within the permissions granted;
- (b) the Firm's use of the Connected Service is governed by its agreement with that provider, and the Firm is responsible for having the rights and client consents needed to move data between them;
- (c) data that the Firm directs Willow to send to a Connected Service leaves Willow's control once delivered, and is not Firm Customer Data processed by Willow under the DPA after that point. The provider of a Connected Service is not Willow's subprocessor;
- (d) Willow uses data from a Connected Service only to provide the features the Firm uses, never for advertising or to train any artificial intelligence model, and in compliance with any additional restrictions the provider imposes, as described in the Privacy Policy;
- (e) Willow is not responsible for the availability, security, accuracy or acts of any Connected Service or its provider, or for changes a provider makes that limit or end an integration; and
- (f) some Connected Services may require Feature Terms or additional fees, which Willow will present before the Firm enables them.

Where the Firm uses the booking features of the Services, the Services determine an Attorney's availability only from the calendars the Attorney has connected, and treat a meeting created through a connected video service as busy time only if it appears on a connected calendar. The Firm is responsible for keeping its connected calendars current.
