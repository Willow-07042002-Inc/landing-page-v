# Willow Consumer Health Data Notice

> **Summary:** Estate planning often involves health information, such as a health care proxy or a living will. Some privacy laws ask companies that handle "consumer health data" to explain, in a separate notice, what health data they handle, why, who sees it and what rights you have. This notice does that for everyone who uses Willow, wherever you live. It adds to our Privacy Policy and does not replace it.

**Effective date:** \[to be set on publication\].

## 1. Who this notice covers

This notice is issued by 07042002 Inc., a Delaware corporation doing business as Willow ("**Willow**," "**we**" or "**us**"). It applies to the consumer health data of Members, Designated Recipients, Fiduciaries and other people named in the documents stored in Willow. Capitalized terms have the meanings given in our Privacy Policy. When a law firm uploads its clients' documents, the firm decides how that information is used and Willow processes it on the firm's behalf; this notice describes what Willow does with it.

## 2. What consumer health data we collect

"**Consumer health data**" means personal information that identifies a person's past, present or future physical or mental health. In Willow it can include:

- health information contained in estate planning documents, such as health care proxies, health care surrogate designations, living wills, advance directives, HIPAA authorizations and the health-related terms of a trust or power of attorney;
- supporting files that a Member or their attorney chooses to upload, such as medical records, insurance policies or a list of medications and physicians;
- answers to check-ins, surveys and life-update questions about a change in health;
- information about a Member's death or incapacity, such as a death certificate or a court order provided with a release request; and
- information about the health of other people named in a plan, such as a child or dependent with special needs.

We do not infer health information from other data, we do not collect precise geolocation, and we do not use any data to identify a person's visits to a health care facility or provider.

## 3. Where it comes from

We receive consumer health data from you, from the attorney or law firm you work with, from the people you choose to share with, and from fiduciaries and others who request a release. AI-generated draft summaries are created by Willow from documents an attorney uploads or manages.

## 4. How we use it

We use consumer health data only to:

- store, organize and display the documents and information in a Member Account;
- share documents and sections of information with the people a Member chooses, according to the Member's Access Instructions;
- analyze documents an attorney uploads or manages, to classify them and prepare draft summaries for the attorney's review, as described in Section 4.2 of the Privacy Policy;
- verify requests to release documents and notify the people involved; and
- provide support, keep the Services secure and comply with law.

We never use consumer health data for marketing or advertising, never sell it, never use it to build profiles or make automated decisions about you, and never use it to train AI models.

## 5. Who we share it with

- **Your attorney and their law firm**, while your Member Account is connected to them, and afterwards in the firm's own record of you as a former client, which it keeps as part of its client file.
- **The people you choose**, and the fiduciaries who pass verification, under your Access Instructions.
- **Service providers** that host and operate the Services under written confidentiality obligations: Supabase, Inc. (on Amazon Web Services) for database, file storage and authentication; Vercel, Inc. for application hosting; Anthropic, PBC for AI analysis of documents an attorney uploads or manages; and Google LLC (Google Workspace) for email delivery. Our subprocessor page at willow-inc.com/subprocessors is the current list.
- **Courts and authorities**, when the law requires it, as described in Section 5.5 of the Privacy Policy.

We have no affiliates, and we do not share consumer health data with anyone else.

## 6. Consent

Using Willow for your estate planning necessarily involves handling the health information in your documents. When you create your account, we ask for your consent to our handling of sensitive information, including health information, separately from your acceptance of the Member Terms. Handling it is also necessary to provide the service you have asked for. You may withdraw that consent at any time by emailing support@willow-inc.com. We will confirm the request with you and explain what it means: because we cannot keep your estate plan without the health information in it, withdrawing consent for particular information means we remove it from your account and stop using it, and withdrawing it altogether means closing your account, after you have had the chance to download your documents. We act within 15 days after we receive your request, and keep only what the law requires us to keep and the records described in Section 8. Where a law firm uploads documents for its clients, the firm obtains any consent its professional obligations require, and we also ask each Member for their own consent when they create their account; the firm's own record of you as its client stays with the firm.

## 7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to:

- confirm whether we are processing your consumer health data, and give you access to it;
- give you a list of the third parties with whom we have shared it (Section 5);
- correct inaccurate information;
- delete your consumer health data, including from our backups as they are overwritten; and
- stop processing it by withdrawing your consent (Section 6).

To make a request, email support@willow-inc.com. We verify your identity first, usually by confirming control of your account or email address, respond within 45 days, and tell you if we need up to 45 more days. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision or emailing support@willow-inc.com with "Appeal" in the subject line, and we will respond in writing within 45 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact your state attorney general. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights. For information a law firm manages about you, we may refer your request to the firm and help the firm respond.

## 8. How long we keep it

Consumer health data is kept as described in Section 6 of the Privacy Policy: while your account is active and for the periods listed there afterwards, so that your fiduciaries can still obtain your documents when they need them.

## 9. Changes and contact

We will post any change to this notice on our website and, for a material change, give notice as described in Section 11 of the Privacy Policy. Questions can be sent to support@willow-inc.com or to Willow (07042002 Inc.), Attn: Privacy contact.
