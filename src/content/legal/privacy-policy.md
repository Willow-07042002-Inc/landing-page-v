# Willow Privacy Policy

Version of November 13, 2026

## 1. Introduction and Scope

> **Summary:** This policy explains what information Willow collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. When a law firm uses Willow for its clients, the firm decides how that information is used and we act on its behalf. The summaries in these boxes are for convenience and are not part of the binding policy.

**Effective date:** \[to be set on publication\]. **Last updated:** \[to be set on publication\].

1.1 **Who we are.** This Privacy Policy is issued by 07042002 Inc., a Delaware corporation doing business as Willow ("**Willow**," "**we**," "**us**" or "**our**"). Willow provides a secure, web-based platform that estate planning attorneys and their clients use to store, organize, analyze, summarize and share copies of estate planning documents, to schedule consultations, and to coordinate the release of documents to designated people (the "**Services**").

1.2 **Who this policy covers.** This policy applies to personal information we process about:

- (a) **Firms** and their **Authorized Users**: law firms that subscribe to the Services, and the attorneys and staff they authorize to use them;
- (b) **Members**: individuals who hold a Willow account for their own estate planning, whether invited by a Firm or signed up directly, and whether or not documents have been added to it yet;
- (c) **Designated Recipients** and **Fiduciaries**: people a Member chooses to share documents with, and executors, personal representatives, trustees, agents under a power of attorney, health care agents, guardians and others in a similar legal role who request documents;
- (d) **Prospective clients** who book a consultation through a Firm's Willow booking page, or who submit a Firm's lead or intake form in Willow;
- (e) **Visitors** to willow-inc.com and our related websites, including people who sign up for updates or early access (such as our Founding 100 program), submit our investor interest or contact forms, or book a demonstration with us; and
- (f) **Business contacts**: attorneys, law firm staff and other professionals whom we contact, or who contact us, about Willow.

1.3 **Our two roles.** When a Firm uploads or manages information about its clients through the Services ("**Firm Customer Data**"), we process that information on the Firm's behalf and under its instructions, as a service provider or processor. The Firm's own privacy notice and its professional obligations, including attorney-client confidentiality, govern the Firm's use of that information. Questions about Firm Customer Data should go first to the Firm. For information we collect directly, such as account details of Members who sign up on their own, website and usage data, business contact information and billing information, Willow decides how the information is used and this policy governs.

1.4 **Not a law firm.** Willow is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Using the Services, booking a consultation or sending a message through Willow does not create an attorney-client relationship with Willow or with any Firm. Such a relationship forms only when you formally engage an attorney.

1.5 **Related terms.** This policy should be read together with the Willow Member Terms and the Willow Firm Subscription Agreement (including its Data Processing Addendum), but it is not part of those agreements. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in those agreements. If this policy conflicts with a Firm's signed agreement or Data Processing Addendum on how Firm Customer Data is handled, the signed agreement controls.

1.6 **Who may use the Services.** The Services are intended for individuals 18 years of age or older who reside in a U.S. state where Willow makes the Services available, as listed on our website (our "**availability page**").

## 2. Information We Collect

> **Summary:** We collect what you give us (your account details, your estate planning documents and your answers to check-ins and surveys), what Firms upload for their clients, what we need to verify people who request documents, limited data from the calendar, contacts and email accounts an attorney connects, payment details through our payment processor, and basic technical data about how the Services are used. The Willow apps use only the cookies they need to work. Our marketing website uses optional analytics only if you agree, and we do not use advertising cookies or targeted advertising anywhere.

2.1 **Information you provide.**

- (a) **Account information:** name, email address, telephone number, state of residence, password (stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider), multi-factor authentication settings, and the record of your acceptance of our terms and consents (what you agreed to, the date, your IP address and browser type). For Attorneys and Authorized Users, we also collect firm name, role, office address, jurisdictions of licensure and professional profile details shown on booking pages.
- (b) **Estate planning content:** copies of wills, trusts, powers of attorney, health care proxies and surrogate designations, living wills, HIPAA authorizations and related documents, together with supporting files a Member or Firm chooses to upload (for example insurance policies, deeds, account lists, school or medical records), notes, and instructions about who should receive which documents and when ("**Access Instructions**").
- (c) **Information about other people:** names, relationships, contact details and roles (such as executor, trustee, agent, guardian or beneficiary) of family members and other people named in a plan or designated to receive documents. If you provide information about another person, you confirm that you are permitted to do so.
- (d) **Booking and intake information:** name, email, telephone number, preferred time and any message a prospective client enters on a Firm's booking page, and the information a prospective client enters on a Firm's lead or intake form in Willow. That information goes to the Firm and is Firm Customer Data. Please do not include confidential details of your legal matter in a booking request.
- (e) **Release and verification information:** when someone requests documents after a Member's death or another release event the Member chose, we collect the requester's name, contact details, relationship to the Member, any note they add, and the information and records the person verifying the request asks for, for example a death certificate, letters testamentary, a court order or identification. The Member's attorney, or Willow where no attorney is connected, decides what is needed.
- (f) **Communications:** messages you send us or send through the Services, support requests and feedback.
- (g) **Check-ins, surveys and life updates:** your answers to check-ins, surveys and life-update questions in the Services, for example about a marriage, a birth, a move or a change in health. If your account is connected to a Firm, the Firm sees your answers so that it can tell whether your plan needs updating. These answers can include sensitive information (Section 2.2).
- (h) **Website forms and demonstrations:** the information you enter when you sign up for updates or early access (such as our Founding 100 program), submit our investor interest or contact forms, or book a demonstration, such as your name, email address, firm or organization and message. We schedule demonstrations through Cal.com, which receives the details you enter to book.

2.2 **Sensitive information.** Estate planning content, supporting files, and answers to check-ins and surveys often contain sensitive personal information, including health information, financial account information, government identifiers, information about minors, and information about a person's death or incapacity. We collect and use sensitive information only to provide the Services you or your Firm have requested, as described in this policy, and never to market to you, to profile you or to make automated decisions about you. When you create a Willow account, we ask for your consent to our handling of this kind of information, separately from your acceptance of the Member Terms. Handling it is also necessary to provide the Services you have asked for: an estate plan cannot be stored, summarized or shared without the health, financial and identifying information it contains. You may withdraw your consent at any time by emailing support@willow-inc.com. We will confirm the request with you, explain what it means and give you the chance to download your documents first, because we cannot keep your estate plan in Willow without this information: withdrawing consent for particular information means we remove it from your account and stop using it, and withdrawing it altogether means closing your account. We act within 15 days after we receive your request. We keep only what the law requires us to keep and the records described in Section 6, and a Firm's own record of you as its client stays with the Firm (Section 5.1). You can also delete files you uploaded yourself at any time in the Services. Where a Firm uploads documents for its clients, the Firm obtains any consent its professional obligations require, and we also ask each Member for their own consent when they create their account. Health information is also covered by our Consumer Health Data Notice, which is published alongside this policy: Consumer Health Data Notice

2.3 **Information from Firms.** A Firm may create a Member account, upload documents and enter information about its clients and their families. When a Firm uploads a document, the Services may also identify the people named in it and use that information to set up a client record for the Firm, which can happen before the client has a Willow account (Section 4.2). That information is Firm Customer Data (Section 1.3).

2.4 **Information from connected accounts.** If an Attorney or Authorized User connects a Google, Microsoft or Zoom account, we receive the limited information described in Section 3.

2.5 **Payment information.** Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not receive or store full card numbers. Stripe gives us limited payment details, such as the type of payment method, the card brand and last four digits, the billing name and address, and a record of each transaction.

We use only cookies and local storage that are strictly necessary to sign you in, keep your session secure, remember a trusted device for multi-factor authentication, and remember basic preferences.

## 3. Connected Services

> **Summary:** You can choose to connect other apps and accounts to Willow, such as your calendar, contacts, email, video meetings or practice management software. We access only what each connection needs for the features you turn on, never use that data for advertising, send it to our AI provider or use it to train AI, and you can disconnect at any time. Our website lists the connections we currently offer and what each one accesses.

3.1 **How connections work.** A "**Connected Service**" is any third-party application, account or data source that you choose to connect to Willow, such as a calendar, email, video conferencing, practice management, document management, database or productivity service. The following rules apply to every Connected Service:

- (a) **Your choice.** Connections are optional. A Connected Service is connected only when you choose to connect it and grant permission on that provider's own authorization screen, and only the features you turn on use it.
- (b) **What we access.** We access, retrieve and send only the data needed for the features you enable, within the permissions you grant. Depending on the connection, this may include account identifiers, contacts, calendar availability and events, meeting details, email message details such as sender, recipients, subject and date, client and matter records, documents, and other records you choose to import or export. If a provider grants Willow broader access than a feature needs, for example permission to read the full contents of a mailbox, we do not use it, and we ask you to reconnect with only the permissions the feature needs.
- (c) **How we use it.** We use data from a Connected Service only to provide and improve the features you use, and never for advertising, to sell it, or to train or improve any artificial intelligence model. We do not send calendar, email or meeting data from a Connected Service to our AI provider.
- (d) **Data you send out.** When you direct Willow to send information to a Connected Service, that provider receives it under its own terms and privacy policy, which we do not control.
- (e) **What we keep.** We store access credentials (tokens) for as long as the connection is active, and data you import into Willow becomes part of your account. We do not keep a separate copy of a Connected Service's data beyond what a feature needs.
- (f) **Disconnecting.** You can disconnect any Connected Service at any time in your connection settings in the Services, by contacting us, or through the provider. When you disconnect through Willow, we stop accessing the Connected Service, delete the stored credentials and, where the provider allows it, ask the provider to cancel Willow's access. If you remove Willow's access through the provider instead, or the connection expires, Willow can no longer access it; we delete the stored credentials once we learn of it, keep your settings, and ask you to reconnect if you want to use the feature again. Data already imported into Willow stays in your account until you delete it.
- (g) **Current connections.** We list the Connected Services we offer, and what each one accesses, on our website and when you connect it. We may add, change or retire connections over time. Where a provider requires specific disclosures in this policy, they appear below.

At present, only Attorneys and Authorized Users can connect a third-party account. If we offer connections to Members in the future, this Section 3 will apply to them in the same way.

3.4 **Microsoft data.** If you connect a Microsoft 365 or Outlook account, Willow requests permission to sign you in and read your basic profile (User.Read, openid, email), to keep the connection active (offline\_access), to read basic details of your email messages (Mail.ReadBasic), and to read the list of people you work and email with (People.Read), which includes your Outlook contacts. If you also connect your Outlook calendar, which is a separate connection, Willow requests permission to read and write your calendars (Calendars.ReadWrite). Willow uses these permissions only to:

- (a) check your free and busy times for your booking page, and add consultations booked through Willow to your calendar, with a Microsoft Teams meeting link if you choose one;
- (b) show you, on a client's record in Willow, your email correspondence with that client from the last year. For this, Willow reads only the sender, recipients, subject and date of your Outlook messages, live from Microsoft each time you view them; and
- (c) when you add a client, find their contact details: Willow searches the people you email most, and, from up to the last three years, the titles and guest lists of your calendar events and the sender, recipients and subject lines of your email messages, for the client's name, and suggests an email address and, where Outlook has one, a phone number. It never reads the contents of your email. Suggestions are shown only to you, and nothing is stored unless you save them to the client record.

Willow never reads the body or attachments of your email messages, and the Mail.ReadBasic permission does not allow it. If a Microsoft account is connected with a broader permission than those listed, such as Mail.Read, Willow does not use it and asks you to reconnect with the permissions listed. The message and calendar details described in (b) and (c) are read live and never stored. Willow stores only the connection tokens, the connected account's email address, the IDs of events it created, and the contact details you choose to save to a client record. Willow does not use Microsoft data for advertising or marketing, does not send it to any AI provider, and does not use it to train any AI model.

3.5 **Zoom data.** If you connect a Zoom account, Willow requests permission to read your basic Zoom user profile (user:read) and to create meetings (meeting:write). Willow uses them only to create a Zoom meeting link for a consultation booked through Willow. Willow stores your Zoom user ID, your Zoom account email and the join link of each meeting it creates, and never reads your Zoom meetings or recordings. If you remove Willow inside your Zoom account, Zoom tells us, and we delete your stored Zoom sign-in and switch the Zoom connection off in Willow. Your availability comes only from the calendars you have connected; a Zoom meeting affects it only if it appears on one of those calendars.

3.6 **Storage and security.** Sign-in credentials (access and refresh tokens) for connected accounts are kept in restricted storage that only Willow's servers can read, encrypted at rest by our database provider, and are used only to perform the features above. Data from connected accounts is processed in the United States.

3.7 **Disconnecting.** You can disconnect Google, Microsoft or Zoom at any time in your connection settings in the Services or by contacting us. When you do, Willow stops accessing the account, deletes the stored sign-in and, for Google and Zoom, asks the provider to cancel Willow's access. Microsoft does not let apps do this, so we tell you how to remove Willow from your Microsoft account yourself. You can also remove Willow's access directly in your [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), Microsoft account settings or the Zoom App Marketplace. If a connection expires or is revoked, Willow deletes the stored sign-in, keeps your settings and asks you to reconnect. Events Willow already added to your calendar, and contact details you saved to a client record, stay where they are until you delete them.

3.8 **New connections.** Adding a Connected Service that follows Section 3.1 does not change this policy; we update the list on our website, and you consent when you choose to connect it. If we want to use data from a Connected Service in a way not described in this Section 3, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

## 4. How We Use Information

> **Summary:** We use information to run the Services, keep them secure, verify people before releasing documents, bill for the Services, communicate with you and meet legal obligations. We use AI only to analyze documents an attorney uploads or manages and to prepare drafts for the attorney's review, and no AI model is trained on your documents. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

4.1 **Purposes.** We use personal information to:

- (a) create and administer accounts, authenticate users and provide the features requested;
- (b) store, organize, display and share documents according to a Member's instructions, a Firm's instructions and the Access Instructions;
- (c) analyze documents and supporting files an Attorney uploads or manages, to classify them, identify the people and key terms in them, set up client records and prepare draft summaries for the Attorney's review (Section 4.2);
- (d) verify requests to release documents, as the Member's attorney or Willow decides is needed, and notify the Firms and people involved as the release process requires;
- (e) schedule consultations, show attorney availability, create calendar events and video meeting links, help Attorneys fill in a client's contact details, and show Attorneys their email correspondence with a client (Section 3);
- (f) bill for the Services, process payments, send receipts and renewal notices, and manage subscriptions;
- (g) send service communications, such as security alerts, renewal reminders, plan-review reminders, and messages, check-ins and surveys that a Firm sends to its clients through the Services;
- (h) provide support and respond to requests;
- (i) monitor, secure and improve the reliability and performance of the Services, and detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse and security incidents;
- (j) comply with law, legal process and professional obligations, and establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
- (k) communicate with attorneys, law firms and others about Willow, including people who sign up for updates, submit a form or book a demonstration on our website, and the business contacts described in Section 2.7; and
- (l) carry out any other purpose disclosed to you at the time of collection, with your consent where required.

4.2 **Artificial intelligence.** Willow uses a large language model provided by Anthropic, PBC to analyze estate planning documents and supporting files that an Attorney uploads or manages: to classify them, identify the people and key terms in them, and prepare draft summaries for the Attorney's review. For example, when an Attorney uploads a document, the Services may identify the type of document, the people who made it, their spouse and their address, and use that information to set up a client record for the Attorney, which can happen before the client has a Willow account. The Services may also identify trust terms, describe "what-if" scenarios and summarize individual sections. These results are drafts for the Attorney's review and are not legal advice, and a Member sees an AI summary only after the Attorney approves it and only while the Member has an active plan. Specifically:

- (a) we send the uploaded document to Anthropic through its commercial API, under terms that prohibit Anthropic from using that content to train its models;
- (b) Willow does not train or fine-tune any AI model on Member documents, summaries or Firm Customer Data;
- (c) Willow improves its document analysis by refining its own library of instructions and summary components, which may be informed by the kinds of edits Attorneys make, but which do not contain the content of any Member's documents;
- (d) documents in accounts that have no Attorney are not analyzed with AI; and
- (e) Anthropic processes document content only to return the results of the analysis to Willow, under contract terms that require it to keep the content confidential. Any temporary retention by Anthropic, for example to monitor for misuse or to comply with law, is governed by those terms. We describe our current configuration on our subprocessor page.

4.3 **Aggregated and de-identified information.** We may create aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be linked to any individual or Firm, and use it to understand and improve the Services. We will not attempt to re-identify it.

4.4 **What we do not do.** We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, use it for targeted advertising or use it to build advertising profiles, anywhere. We do not use estate planning content, Firm Customer Data or data from Connected Services for marketing. We do not use personal information to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

## 5. How We Share Information

> **Summary:** We share information with your Firm, with the people you choose, with Fiduciaries whose requests have been verified under your Access Instructions (by your attorney, or by Willow if you have none), and with vendors who help us run the Services under written confidentiality obligations. We disclose information for legal reasons only when we believe the law requires it or it is necessary to protect someone's safety, address fraud or security, or protect Willow's rights, and we tell you first where we can. We do not sell personal information.

5.1 **With your Firm and Attorney.** If a Member's account is connected to a Firm, the Firm's Attorneys and Authorized Users can access that Member's documents, summaries, answers to check-ins and surveys, and account activity as needed to provide legal services, but not files the Member uploads themselves or personal contacts the Member has not chosen to share with the Firm. If the connection ends, for example because the Member disconnects or the Firm stops using Willow, the Firm can no longer see the Member's account, including anything added afterwards, although the Firm keeps its own record of the Member as a former client, as attorneys are required to do: the Member's contact details, the documents and approved summaries that were in the Member's account (including any from an earlier Firm), the Member's team and, if the Member shared them, contacts, the Firm's own notes, and the messages, check-ins and surveys the Firm sent. Willow holds that record for the Firm as part of the Firm's client file, and requests about it go to the Firm (Section 12.2). A Member is connected to one Firm at a time. If the Member later accepts a new Firm, that Firm sees the Member's whole account, including documents and summaries from an earlier Firm, because the Member's acceptance is their direction to share it. The Member Terms describe what else happens when a connection ends.

5.2 **With people you choose.** When a Member shares a document or a section of information, we show it to the people the Member chose, and to no one else. When a Firm directs us to share its data, we follow the Firm's instructions. If an Attorney links two Members' accounts as a couple, linking does not by itself share their documents: each sees the other's name, email address and whether they are connected to a Firm, and one couples Plan covers both; a document the Attorney identifies as joint is placed in both accounts; anything else is shared only if that Member chooses to share it. People you share with never pay to see what you shared with them (a release request for an account whose plan has lapsed may carry a reasonable fee, shown before the request is submitted, as the Member Terms describe), and they accept the Recipient Terms before their first access.

5.3 **With Fiduciaries under Access Instructions.** Members choose, document by document and section by section, who may see what. Powers of attorney and health care documents are shared with the named agents when the Member chooses to share them. A will may be shared so that it stays locked until the Member's death, and is unlocked only after a request is verified by the Member's attorney of record, under the attorney's own procedures, or, where there is none, by Willow. Requesters provide the proof the verifier asks for, which may include proof of identity and of the death or other release event. A Member may also choose other release events if Willow offers them, and those releases are verified in the same way. Willow does not make legal or medical determinations, such as whether a Member has capacity. We may also release documents to a court-appointed personal representative on presentation of letters or a court order. A Member's sharing choices ("**Access Instructions**") are given through a tool the Member can change at any time, and serve as the Member's direction to Willow under the law of the Member's state on fiduciary access to digital assets. We keep a record of every release.
